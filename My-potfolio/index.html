✦ About Me

Who I Am

Hi! I'm Solongo — a student at MAIS with a deep love for business studies, chemistry, and the intersections between them. I believe in learning by building: whether that means teaching others a tiny piece of what I have learned and gained , diving into a real life problem, or dreaming up a new project.
